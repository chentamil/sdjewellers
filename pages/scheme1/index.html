Jewellery Chit Scheme

Save monthly towards your jewellery purchase through our 11-month chit scheme.

Scheme Details

  • The chit scheme runs for 11 months.
  • Members may purchase jewellery using the amount accumulated under the scheme.
  • Each member may purchase jewellery or make an eligible advance booking in accordance with the scheme terms.
  • Members can contribute a monthly instalment of ₹1,000 or multiples thereof, subject to the scheme's applicable rules.
  • Eligible instalments include ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000 and other permitted multiples.
  • The accumulated amount may be used towards jewellery purchases, subject to the scheme conditions.
  • Members should complete the required registration and documentation.
  • Scheme benefits and jewellery pricing are subject to the terms described below.

Terms and Conditions

  1. The scheme period is 11 months.
  2. Members may remit their instalments according to the scheme schedule.
  3. Jewellery purchases must comply with the scheme's eligibility and purchase conditions.
  4. Benefits are subject to the applicable government rules and regulations.
  5. No interest is payable on the accumulated scheme amount, as stated in the notice.
  6. Members must retain their chit or membership records and provide them when required.
  7. The scheme passbook or membership record must be presented when making a claim or purchase, where applicable.
  8. Scheme records must be maintained properly by the member.
  9. Scheme rules and benefits are subject to the applicable terms and conditions.
  10. Members who complete all 11 monthly instalments may receive the eligible scheme benefits after completion, subject to the scheme rules.
  11. If the scheme is discontinued or cancelled before completion, benefits may be affected according to the applicable terms.
  12. Jewellery purchased under the scheme will be valued according to the applicable purchase terms.
  13. GST and other applicable statutory charges are payable as required by law.
  14. Additional terms may apply to high-value jewellery purchases and eligible scheme benefits.
  15. All transactions are subject to applicable government regulations.

Please contact our jewellery shop for the official scheme rules, current gold rates, applicable charges and membership details.
